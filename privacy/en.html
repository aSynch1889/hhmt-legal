This policy describes how information relates to hhmt. All data processing happens 100% on your device. In-app purchases are processed by Apple.

2. How we handle your data

2.1 On-device processing

Vitals you enter manually (blood pressure, glucose, SpO₂, weight, water, temperature), and experimental pulse / HRV estimates from the fingertip PPG feature (rear camera + flash) are all recorded, analyzed, and charted on your iPhone/iPad. Your data is not uploaded to cloud servers we operate, and the app works fully offline.

2.2 Data storage

  • Health records are stored locally in the app's protected Application Support/HealthRecords directory. The record directory and its backup file are excluded from iCloud backup;
  • Language, appearance, reminder, and other app preferences are stored locally using system preferences;
  • PDF reports you actively export are written to a destination you choose via the system share sheet (Files, AirDrop, email, other apps, etc.);
  • This data is not automatically synced to servers we operate.

3. Permissions

  • Camera (NSCameraUsageDescription): used for an experimental pulse estimate via the fingertip PPG feature (rear camera and flash) — only when you actively use it;
  • Apple Health (HealthKit): used only to write supported manual records (heart rate, blood pressure, oxygen saturation, weight, and water) when you enable saving. The app does not read HealthKit history or automatically sync records. Experimental camera PPG results are not written to HealthKit.

These permissions are managed by iOS and can be reviewed or revoked anytime in system Settings. The camera feed is used only for live measurement/recognition and is not saved or uploaded. We do not use these permissions for tracking or to build an identity profile of you.

4. What we do not collect

  • No name, phone number, contacts, or precise location;
  • No advertising or third-party tracking SDKs / IDFA advertising tracking;
  • No sign-in is required for core features;
  • We do not receive your health records on our servers. If you export/share a PDF, iOS sends it to the destination you choose.

5. In-app purchases

hhmt Premium (monthly, yearly auto-renewable, or one-time lifetime — no weekly plan) is processed by Apple via App Store / StoreKit. Payment, billing, refunds, and subscription management follow Apple's terms. We cannot read your full card details from the app.

6. Crash & usage analytics

The current version does not integrate third-party crash/analytics SDKs that report data back to us. If this changes in the future, we will update this page and comply with applicable law.

7. Retention & deletion

Your measurement records remain on your device until you delete individual records or uninstall the app (subject to iOS behavior). Because we do not host your health data, there is generally no cloud-data deletion request needed. The app currently does not provide a bulk clear-all action; uninstalling the app removes its local data. Emails you send us are handled under ordinary email practices.

8. Children

The app is intended for general users who wish to log blood pressure and pulse, and is not directed at children under 13 for collecting personal information.

9. Medical disclaimer

hhmt provides tracking and reference metrics only. It is not a medical device, and its measurements and stage classifications cannot replace professional medical diagnosis. PPG pulse measurement is an estimate and may differ from medical-grade devices. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider for any health concerns or medical decisions.

10. Third-party services

The app may invoke iOS system capabilities such as sharing, camera, and photo library, all managed by Apple. In-app purchases are processed by Apple.
